# Main figure legends (draft)

## Figure 1. Study design for reliability evaluation of deep generative single-cell multi-omics models.
Overview of datasets, models, and stress tests. PBMC CITE-seq cohorts (PBMC10k, PBMC5k) were used for representation benchmarking, entry-wise protein corruption, bidirectional cross-dataset transfer, uncertainty characterization, and replicated stochastic-variability analyses. An independent Lawlor Baseline CITE-seq cohort (10 donors) provided donor-held-out external validation with author labels. Models included PCA (RNA), MOFA+, architecture-matched scVI (scVI_matched), and totalVI. Uncertainty endpoints separate predictive confidence from latent posterior variance. Selective prediction uses confidence-based abstention with per-class retention reporting. Abbreviations: CITE-seq, cellular indexing of transcriptomes and epitopes by sequencing.

## Figure 2. Biological representation quality and robustness to entry-wise protein corruption.
**(A)** Schematic of unimodal and multimodal representation models. **(B)** Fine-grained (l2) high-confidence held-out logistic-regression macro-F1 for PCA_RNA, MOFA+, scVI_matched, and totalVI on the independently annotated PBMC query (n cells and classes as in `mofa_biological_representation_metrics.csv`). Error bars / intervals follow the bootstrap comparisons in pairwise primary tables (n_boot=500 for totalVI−scVI_matched). **(C)** Mean totalVI−scVI_matched macro-F1 difference under entry-wise protein count corruption at fractions 0–0.85 (mask seeds as in PHASE 8 summary). Positive values indicate multimodal advantage relative to the fixed scVI_matched reference. This experiment corrupts observed protein entries and is not a missing-modality design. **(D)** Optional class-resolved robustness summary. Batch silhouette is reported in supplement and must not be interpreted as biological quality alone.

## Figure 3. Cross-dataset transfer depends on shift direction and source context.
**(A)** Direction A (PBMC10k→PBMC5k) and Direction B (PBMC5k→PBMC10k) with scArches query adaptation; source and target embeddings are both taken from the post-adaptation model. **(B)** Paired seed comparisons of l2 macro-F1 for scVI_matched vs totalVI. Primary interpretation uses the 20-seed grid (Direction A near null; Direction B favors totalVI in 19/20 seeds). Corrected 5-seed summaries are shown only as historical consistency after fixing mixed-coordinate artifacts. **(C)** Source-size sensitivity under Direction A size-matching. **(D)** Selected class-specific transfer effects. Values are mean ± SD across seeds unless noted; no new p-values were added for manuscript decoration.

## Figure 4. Predictive versus latent uncertainty under dataset shift.
**(A)** Error-detection AUROC using predictive uncertainty \(U_{\mathrm{pred}}=1-\max_k p(y=k\mid z)\). **(B)** Error-detection AUROC using latent posterior uncertainty \(U_{\mathrm{latent}}=\frac{1}{d}\sum_j\mathrm{Var}[z_j\mid x]\). **(C)** Association between predictive and latent uncertainty. **(D)** Probability-quality and selective-risk summaries (NLL, Brier, AURC). ECE is shown separately when discussed and is not treated as a discrimination metric. Points summarize 20 seeds per direction×model (PHASE 11B). Language distinguishes error ranking, probability quality, and calibration.

## Figure 5. Stochastic fragility, variance decomposition, and selective prediction.
**(A)** Association between full-model uncertainty and prediction instability across source-subset and/or seed perturbations. **(B)** Replicated variance decomposition for key metrics under a balanced source-subset × model-seed × run-replicate design (Direction A): source main effect, seed main effect, interaction, and run-level residual. Residual denotes stochastic/run variability, not irreducible biological noise. **(C)** Risk–coverage curves under confidence abstention. **(D)** Cell-type retention at reduced coverage, highlighting coverage imbalance across biological classes. PBMC panels use PHASE 11B; variance panels use PHASE 12A.

## Figure 6. Independent donor-held-out validation on Lawlor Baseline CITE-seq.
**(A)** Ten donors assigned to five deterministic two-donor target folds; batch covariate is sequencing lane (`run_identifier`), not donor. Analysis uses Baseline singlets (16,175), author-labeled cells for supervised evaluation (5,207), 12,776 shared genes, and a 12-protein panel. **(B)** Donor-held-out macro-F1 for scVI_matched and totalVI (5 folds × 10 seeds). **(C)** Predictive error AUROC. **(D)** Latent error AUROC. **(E)** Risk–coverage. **(F)** Class retention under abstention (e.g., preferential rejection of CD4/CD8 naive and CD4 memory relative to monocytes/B cells). Paired deltas are within fold×seed. Between-fold SD and within-fold seed SD are reported in variability tables. Labels are Lawlor author annotations only.
